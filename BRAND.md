Primary Typeface
Source Serif 4 is our primary brand
typeface and should be used for all
headlines and main titles. It may
also be used for quotes and more
emotive callouts.

Secondary Typeface
Inter is our secondary typeface which
should be used for all body copy, as well
as subheadings, numbers, statistics,
graphs and callouts where the legibility
and functionality of type is paramount

Charts and graphs should be kept simple
and clean for clarity and ease of reading.
The Ventia primary colour palette should
always be used as the lead colours.
Secondary colours can be used in charts
and graphs if required, as shown.
